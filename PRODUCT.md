# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + Tailwind, static output. Content in typed data files. Hosting undecided (must work on GitHub Pages, Vercel or Netlify).

## Users

Equal priority: recruiters and hiring managers at AI/ML and software companies, fellow engineers judging craft, and potential freelance clients. All arrive from a link (CV, LinkedIn, GitHub, DM) and decide within about 30 seconds whether this person is worth a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A one-page personal site for a software engineer working in AI/ML. It shows who they are, their career path, and their projects, and makes contacting them easy. Success: visitors remember it, understand what the owner does, and reach out.

## Positioning

An engineer who builds ML systems and loves games and anime. The site should show taste and engineering craft, not just list facts. Inspiration is the spirit of science-driven building (from the anime Dr. Stone): progress from first principles, one discovery unlocking the next. No characters, names, art or quotes from that show.

## Operating Context

Visited on desktop and phone, often straight from a recruiter pipeline or social link. Skimmed, not studied.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: intro, about, career timeline, projects, contact (email, GitHub, LinkedIn, CV download).
- Dark only.
- Readable first: everything reachable without interaction; motion adds feel, never gates content.
- Animation is wanted and should be well-crafted; must respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Design is previewed in Figma before code.

## Brand Commitments

- Copy is short and rewritten, never pasted from the CV or LinkedIn. The owner said "don't speak a lot".
- Avoid gimmicky or cringe touches. The first chalkboard concept (chalk/handwriting fonts, XP bar, achievement toasts, jokey equations and copy) was rejected as not good enough.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet in the repo. The owner will provide a CV, LinkedIn text, GitHub username, and possibly a photo. Do not invent employers, metrics or projects in the final site; placeholders must be clearly marked.

## Product Principles

1. Understood in 30 seconds: role, focus and best work visible without scrolling far.
2. Craft over decoration: quality shows in type, spacing and motion, not ornaments.
3. Personality through restraint: interests (ML, games, anime) show up as a subtle point of view, not as theme-park props.
4. Few words, all true.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme, keyboard navigable, reduced-motion respected.
